THE QUICK START

Small square. Big moves.

  • ← / → or A / D — Move left / right
  • Drag finger on the canvas — Move the square (mobile)
  • P or the Pause button — Pause / resume
  • Enter — Confirm username
  • Esc — Close the current dialog
  • E — Toggle error banner

Know your power-ups

  • ❤ 3 Lives — brief invincibility after each hit
  • 🛡 Shield — golden diamond, 6s full protection
  • ⏱ Slow — blue diamond, 5s obstacle slow-motion
  • ✕2 Multiplier — purple diamond, 8s double score
  • 💗 Life — red diamond, restores 1 life
  • FAST blocks (yellow) — smaller but 2.5× speed
  • WIDE blocks (indigo) — very wide but slower
  • ZIGZAG blocks (orange) — bounce side-to-side
  • Near-miss bonus: +25 pts for very close dodges
  • Combo system: survive longer for a score multiplier
  • 🤖 autoplay cheat — AI takes the wheel and dodges for you
  • Type disable autoplay in the cheat bar to take back control
